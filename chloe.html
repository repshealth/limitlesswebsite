Hey, I'm Chlo

YOUR ELITE PREP COACH

Sport has always been a huge part of my life, from horse riding and hockey to beginning my bodybuilding journey in Athletic Figure. In 2022, I transitioned into Wellness, winning both my regional show and Pro Qualifier in my debut season, earning my IFBB Pro Card.

Since then, I've achieved top-four placings on the IFBB Pro stage in 2026, with my sights firmly set on Olympia qualification.

Over the years, I've coached countless women through significant physique development, from building muscle and developing their foundations to achieving top placings on stage. My expertise lies in Bikini and Wellness competition preparation, with an in-depth understanding of the muscular development, conditioning and presentation required to build a truly competitive physique.

My coaching approach combines precision, individualisation and attention to detail with something I believe is equally important: trust and communication. Prep is demanding, both physically and mentally, and I pride myself on building strong relationships with my athletes, where honesty, openness and mutual respect are just as important as the work we put in.
