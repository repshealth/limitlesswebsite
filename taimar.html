Hey, I'm Taimar

YOUR PHYSIQUE COACH

I've been coaching women since 2017, and over that time I've helped clients through countless different stages of their physique journeys, from first stepping into a gym to major body transformations, photoshoots and stepping on stage.

I've also lived a lot of that journey myself. I've competed in Bikini, transformed my own physique, navigated pregnancy and postpartum, significant health complications, operations, injuries and periods where training has had to take a back seat. Those experiences have shaped the coach I am today and taught me that great coaching isn't about applying the same formula to everyone.

My passion is physique development: intelligent training, execution, nutrition and building muscle in the right places to genuinely change someone's shape. I work with women who want more than simply seeing a smaller number on the scales, whether that means taking their physique to another level, preparing for a photoshoot or working towards their first Bikini or Wellness competition.

The goal is always to create a plan specific to the person in front of me, their lifestyle and the physique we're trying to build.
